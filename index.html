What does “total cost” mean?

The fee is only one component. The exchange rate also affects the amount delivered. In a real comparison, the best starting point is the verified amount the provider says the recipient will receive, under the same transfer conditions.

Destinations and data limits

  • Destination selector: Kenya, Egypt, South Africa, Tanzania and Nigeria.
  • Nigeria is a candidate corridor, not a verified “top” Ireland-to-Africa corridor.
  • All sample fees and rates are fictional; provider support for each corridor is unverified.
  • Changing inputs recalculates demonstration scenarios only; no live quote is requested.
  • No money is transferred and no personal information is sent anywhere.
